About the Old Age Pension Scheme

Implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Purvanchal, under the State Social Assistance Programme.

Monthly Benefit
₹250 / Month
Credited via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
Statutory SLA
15 Calendar Days
Right to Public Services Act guarantee

Eligibility Requirements

  • Age: Must be 60 years of age or older on the date of application.
  • Residence: Resident of Purvanchal in designated blocks: Sonari, Rajapara, Dhemaji Pathar, Borgaon, Namti, or Khelua.
  • Bank Account: Active individual savings bank account with a valid 11-character RBI IFSC code.
  • Document: Exactly one valid age proof document (Voter ID Card, Birth Certificate, School Leaving Certificate, or Aadhaar).

Statutory Guarantee (Deemed Approval)

Under the Purvanchal Right to Public Services (RTPS) Act, any application not processed by the Block Welfare Office within the statutory 15-day period stands automatically APPROVED by law. The nightly deemed-approval engine certifies these cases and notifies beneficiaries via SMS.

One Active Application Rule & Correction

An applicant may hold at most one active application (Pending, Approved, or Deemed Approved) at any time. If an applicant realizes they made an error (such as a wrong bank account number), they may withdraw their pending application from the status portal or through their AI assistant and submit a fresh application immediately.

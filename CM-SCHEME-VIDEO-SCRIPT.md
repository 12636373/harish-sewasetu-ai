# Chief Minister's Welfare Innovation Proposal
## Video Script: Mukhyamantri Gramin Sanjivani (2 Minutes &middot; Face to Camera)

> **Role:** Build for Bharat Fellow (AI Track)  
> **Target Audience:** Hon'ble Chief Minister & Department of Social Welfare, Government of Purvanchal  
> **Video Format:** 2-minute video, face to camera, uploaded as Unlisted on YouTube.

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] The Core Motivation: Why I Care
* **Visual:** Face to camera, sincere, direct, and grounded.
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Namaskar Hon'ble Chief Minister Sir and senior leadership.
  >
  > Last month, while visiting a tea garden estate in Upper Purvanchal, I met 76-year-old Rupeswar Tanti. He had missed his monthly pension for four straight months simply because the local bank branch was 22 kilometers away across a flooded river, and his fingerprint failed on an aging biometric scanner.
  >
  > In public governance, our greatest tragedy is not lack of welfare funding—it is the last-mile friction of administrative access. An elderly citizen should never have to wage a physical struggle against rivers, buses, and digital illiteracy to claim what is rightfully theirs by law."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:20] The Scheme: Mukhyamantri Gramin Sanjivani
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"I propose **Mukhyamantri Gramin Sanjivani**—a proactive, agentic welfare and doorstep health delivery initiative for elderly citizens aged 70 and above.
  >
  > Here is how it operates:
  >
  > 1. **Proactive Entitlement Inversion:** Instead of waiting for an illiterate senior citizen to find our web portal and apply, our state civil registration database detects when a resident turns 60. The system automatically drafts their pension case and triggers a home-verification task.
  > 2. **Voice-First Village Agents:** Equipped with lightweight tablets, local ASHA workers and Gaonburhas visit the elder's doorstep. The elder speaks naturally in their mother tongue—Assamese, Bodo, or local dialects. An on-device AI assistant translates their voice, confirms identity via iris recognition, and captures bank passbook details without typing.
  > 3. **Integrated Health & Pension DBT:** Along with ₹250 monthly pension credited directly to Jan Dhan accounts, the scheme bundles monthly doorstep delivery of essential chronic medications (hypertension, diabetes, joint care) delivered by mobile health vans."*

---

### [1:20 – 1:50] The Technology & Accountability Safeguards
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Because we built this on the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and open API standards, the entire pipeline is tamper-proof:
  >
  > - Every visit and doorstep verification is cryptographically signed and logged into our state audit ledger.
  > - If an application is not cleared within our statutory 15-day SLA under the Right to Public Services Act, the pension is automatically deemed approved by law—zero bureaucratic harassment.
  > - No commercial middleman or vendor lock-in. The software runs on our state servers with zero citizen biometric data leaked to external LLMs."*

---

### [1:50 – 2:00] Closing Vision
* **Spoken Script:**
  > *"Hon'ble Chief Minister Sir, true technology does not expect an 80-year-old grandmother to become digitally literate; it makes governance humble enough to walk up to her front door. Mukhyamantri Gramin Sanjivani will make Purvanchal the first state in India where dignity is proactively delivered.
  >
  > Thank you, and Jai Hind."*
